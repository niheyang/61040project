Alice and Bob create accounts and exchange usernames. While Bob’s receiving window is closed, Alice sends him a non-urgent message. Alice can see that her message is waiting, but Bob sees no pending content or waiting-message count. When Bob clicks Catch up now, a 15-minute receiving window opens and Alice’s message appears with its original timestamp. New messages sent during the window becomes available automatically. Bob ends catch-up. Previously released messages remain accessible, while new messages wait until he opens another window. This lets Alice share immediately while Bob controls when new messages become visible.
